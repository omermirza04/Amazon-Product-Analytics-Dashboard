# Amazon Product Analytics Dashboard

Interactive dashboard analyzing 1.35K+ Amazon products and 27M+ ratings to uncover pricing trends and customer sentiment.

## 📊 Dashboard Views

1. **Overview** – Category-wise average ratings, rating counts, and discount vs. rating scatter analysis
2. **Pricing & Discount Analysis** – Actual vs. discounted prices by category, discount bucket distribution, top discounted products
3. **Product Performance** – Top products by rating and review count, detailed product table

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Average product rating: **4.10** – strong overall customer satisfaction
- Average discount: **47.69%** – discount-heavy pricing strategy
- Electronics leads in rating volume and pricing activity
- Office Products, Toys & Games, and Home Improvement tie for highest avg rating (4.3)

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI (data modeling, DAX measures, interactive visuals)

## 📁 Dataset

Amazon products dataset with fields: product name, category, actual price, discounted price, discount %, rating, rating count.
